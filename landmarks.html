Beyond the obvious

Parks & landmarks

10 national parks, viewpoints, and other nature and heritage spots across Satun province — the lesser-known side of the province.

10 spots listed Off the beaten path

Parks & landmarks

Prasat Hin Panyod

La-ngu Boat, tide-dependent

Best Time to Visit: November to April (to enjoy emerald-green water and pleasant weather)

What to Know:

  • A unique limestone formation resembling a castle.
  • Accessible by local longtail boats.
  • Wear comfortable shoes for walking on rugged terrain.

Thale Ban National Park

Khuan Don By road

Best Time to Visit: Year-round

What to Know:

  • Features lush mountains, large wetlands, and rare wildlife like serows.
  • Open daily roughly 08:00–16:30; entry fee is required (foreigner and Thai-national rates differ, as at most Thai national parks).

Jabang Channel

Mueang Satun Boat or snorkel tour

Best Time to Visit: November to April

What to Know:

  • Renowned for vibrant coral reefs.
  • Snorkeling equipment is available for rent on-site.

Mu Ko Phetra National Park

La-ngu By road to the visitor centre; boat for outer islands

Best Time to Visit: November to April (calm seas for boat access)

What to Know:

  • A marine national park spanning about 30 limestone islands between Trang and Satun, with a wooden boardwalk out over the cliffs near the mainland visitor centre.
  • Most of the outer islands have no facilities or regular boat service—visits typically need to be arranged through a local tour operator.
  • Park entry fees apply; a Satun Geopark passport (stamped free at the Geopark gateway centre) gives a discount across Tarutao, Thale Ban, and Mu Ko Phetra.

Thung Nui Hot Spring

Khuan Kalong By road, about 45–60 min from Satun town

Best Time to Visit: Year-round, cooler evenings recommended

What to Know:

  • A natural hot spring averaging around 52°C at the source, in Khuan Kalong District—separate soaking pools are available for men and women.
  • Run by the Satun Provincial Administrative Organisation; open daily 05:00–20:00. Entry: 100 THB adults, 60 THB children.
  • About 45–60 minutes' drive from Satun town, inland from the coast.

Pakbara Viewpoint

La-ngu By road or scooter, short climb

Best Time to Visit: Late afternoon, for sunset over the pier

What to Know:

  • A short scooter or car ride up the hill above Pak Bara Pier, with a wide view over the harbour and the islands beyond.
  • A convenient stop before or after catching a ferry to Ko Lipe or Ko Tarutao from the pier below.

Khao To Ngai — "Bridge Across Time"

La-ngu By road

Best Time to Visit: Late afternoon for sunset

What to Know:

  • A limestone headland in La-ngu District, near Mu Ko Phetra National Park, where the cliff faces on either side show visibly different rock colours from different geological eras—hence the "bridge across time" name.
  • A small café built out over the water nearby makes it a popular sunset stop.

Viewpoint of Koh Adang

Mueang Satun By road

Location: G8G4+4M8, Ko Tarutao, Mueang Satun District, Satun 91000, Thailand

Details:

  • A hilltop viewpoint on Ko Adang (within Tarutao National Park) reached by a short trek, looking out over Ko Lipe and the surrounding Andaman islands.

Chado Cliff Viewpoint

Mueang Satun By road

Location: G876+PRJ, Ko Adang, Mueang Satun District, Satun 91000, Thailand

Details:

  • A series of three numbered clifftop viewpoints on Ko Adang (Tarutao National Park), reached via a forested hiking trail with rope sections, with sweeping views over Ko Lipe.

Ban Tha Oi Viewpoint

Thung Wa By road

Location: Thung Wa, Thung Wa District, Satun 91120, Thailand

Details:

  • A riverside viewpoint and pier area in Thung Wa district overlooking mangroves and a fishing village, part of the Satun UNESCO Global Geopark trail.
