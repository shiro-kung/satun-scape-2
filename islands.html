Boat-hop the Andaman Sea

Islands

19 islands of Satun province, from Ko Lipe and Ko Adang to the smaller islands of Tarutao Marine Park—white sand, clear water, and short boat rides between them.

19 spots listed Tarutao Marine Park

Islands

Koh Lipe

Mueang Satun Speedboat from Pak Bara Pier (~1–1.5 hrs)

Best Time to Visit: November to April (calm seas and clear water)

What to Know:

  • Famous for snorkeling, diving, and vibrant coral reefs.
  • Accessible by speedboat from Pak Bara Pier.
  • Entry to Tarutao National Park requires a fee (approx. 200 THB for foreigners).

Koh Hin Ngam

Mueang Satun Boat tour from Ko Lipe or Pak Bara

Best Time to Visit: November to April

What to Know:

  • Known for its glossy black pebbles.
  • It’s forbidden to remove any stones as souvenirs.

Koh Hin Son

Mueang Satun Boat tour, part of the Mu Ko Dong group

Best Time to Visit: November to April

What to Know:

  • Features unique balanced rock formations that defy gravity.

Koh Adang

Mueang Satun Speedboat from Pak Bara Pier, ~30 min from Ko Lipe

Best Time to Visit: November to April

What to Know:

  • Ideal for hiking, snorkeling, and beach activities.
  • Offers panoramic views of Koh Lipe from its viewpoint.

Koh Bulon

La-ngu Boat from Pak Bara Pier

Best Time to Visit: November to April

What to Know:

  • A tranquil island perfect for diving and snorkeling.
  • Limited accommodation, so book early.

Koh Khai

Mueang Satun Boat stop between Pak Bara and Ko Lipe

Best Time to Visit: November to April

What to Know:

  • Famous for its natural stone arch, a popular photo spot.
  • Symbolic for lovers, as legend says couples who pass through the arch will have eternal love.

Koh Rawi

Mueang Satun Boat tour from Ko Lipe or Ko Adang

Best Time to Visit: November to April

What to Know:

  • A peaceful island with white sand beaches and clear waters.

Koh Kra

Mueang Satun Kayak or boat from Sunrise Beach, Ko Lipe

Best Time to Visit: November to April

What to Know:

  • Offers stunning 360-degree views and pristine beaches.

Koh Yang

Mueang Satun Boat tour from Ko Lipe

Best Time to Visit: November to April

What to Know:

  • Known for coral reefs and turtle nesting sites.

Koh Rok Roi

Mueang Satun Boat from Ko Lipe

Best Time to Visit: November to April

What to Know:

  • A small island in the Mu Ko Dong group within Tarutao National Park, off Ko Sarai Subdistrict, Mueang Satun District.
  • A short stretch of beach surrounded by clear, emerald-green water—coral is visible from the surface without diving gear.
  • Reachable by boat from Ko Lipe; check sailing times with a local tour operator.

Ko Tarutao

Mueang Satun Boat access — check with a local operator for the current route

Location: Ko Tarutao, Satun, Thailand

Details:

  • The largest island of Tarutao National Park, a former penal colony now known for its forests, limestone cliffs, and quiet beaches such as Ao Molae and Ao Son.
  • Google rating: 4.7 stars.

Ko Sing (เกาะสิงห์)

Mueang Satun Boat access — check with a local operator for the current route

Location: Tambon Ko Tarutao, Amphoe Mueang Satun, Satun 91000, Thailand

Details:

  • A small islet within Tarutao National Park, in the Ko Tarutao subdistrict of Mueang Satun district.

Ko Daeng (เกาะแดง)

Mueang Satun Boat access — check with a local operator for the current route

Location: Tambon Ko Tarutao, Amphoe Mueang Satun, Satun 91000, Thailand

Details:

  • Small islet in the Ko Tarutao subdistrict of Mueang Satun district, part of the Tarutao archipelago cluster.

Ko Laen (เกาะแลน)

Mueang Satun Boat access — check with a local operator for the current route

Location: Tambon Ko Tarutao, Amphoe Mueang Satun, Satun 91000, Thailand

Details:

  • Small islet in the Ko Tarutao subdistrict of Mueang Satun district, part of the Tarutao archipelago cluster.
  • Google rating: 5.0 stars.

Ko Dong / Mu Ko Dong (เกาะดง)

Mueang Satun Boat access — check with a local operator for the current route

Location: near Ko Tarutao, Amphoe Mueang Satun, Chang Wat Satun, Thailand

Details:

  • A small island group near Ko Tarutao known for Ao Ling (Monkey Bay), clear water and snorkeling stops on Tarutao boat tours.
  • Google rating: 4.6 stars.

Ko Pu Yu (เกาะปูยู)

Mueang Satun Boat access — check with a local operator for the current route

Location: Tambon Pu Yu, Amphoe Mueang Satun, Chang Wat Satun, Thailand

Details:

  • A mangrove-fringed limestone island near the Satun mainland, known for the Tham Lod Pu Yu tunnel cave that passes through a karst cliff.

Ko Don (เกาะโดน)

La-ngu Boat access — check with a local operator for the current route

Location: Tambon Pak Nam, Amphoe La-ngu, Chang Wat Satun 91110, Thailand

Details:

  • A small island off the coast near Pak Nam subdistrict in La-ngu district.
  • Google rating: 3.7 stars.

Ko Khamin (เกาะขมิ้น)

Mueang Satun Boat access — check with a local operator for the current route

Location: Tambon Pu Yu, Amphoe Mueang Satun, Satun 91000, Thailand

Details:

  • A small island near Ko Pu Yu in Mueang Satun district, used as a viewpoint stop.
  • Google rating: 5.0 stars.

Ko Li Di Lek

Mueang Satun Boat access — check with a local operator for the current route

Location: Ko Li Di Lek, off Mueang Satun District, Satun Province (near Ko Bulon / Ko Sarai waters)

Details:

  • A small, undeveloped island with a roughly 400 m virgin beach of pale sand and turquoise, gradually-shelving water, backed by hills and greenery. Accessible only by boat; minimal facilities, popular for kayaking and quiet swimming.
