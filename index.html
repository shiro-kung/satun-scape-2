Plan your visit to Satun

About the province

Satun is Thailand’s southwestern province along the Andaman Sea, next to Malaysian Perlis / Langkawi. Expect laid-back towns, Malay-Thai cultural mix, limestone islands—and far fewer crowds than hotspots like Phuket or Krabi.

Don’t miss

  • Tarutao National Park: Archipelago cruise, snorkeling, jungle trails—booking ahead helps in high season.
  • Kuden Mansion (museum): Colonial-era house and provincial museum—easy stop in downtown Satun.
  • Phu Pha Phet Cave: One of Thailand’s largest cave systems—wear grippy shoes; guides available on site.
  • Wang Sai Thong Waterfall: Limestone terraces and pools—a classic nature day trip inland.

Food travellers love

Southern curries are bold—many spots offer halal options. Try fresh squid, crab, grilled fish by the waterfront, rotating snack boats, and iced coffee paired with flaky roti in town.

Quick tips

  • Weather: November–April is drier for boats and trekking; storms can disrupt ferries Jul–Oct.
  • Getting around: Songthaews, taxis,Grab where available—or rent a car/scooter if you hold a valid license & IDP.
  • Courtesy: Temples & mosques: cover shoulders/knees; remove shoes where signed; drones need permits at parks.
