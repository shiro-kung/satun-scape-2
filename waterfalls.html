Waterfalls

11 jungle waterfalls across Satun province, fed by the rainy season and best visited July–October.

11 spots listed Best Jul–Oct

Waterfalls

Wang Sai Thong Waterfall

La-ngu By road

Best Time to Visit: July to October (rainy season for golden water cascades)

What to Know:

  • Features unique limestone formations with golden reflections.

Tharn Pliew Waterfall

Thung Wa By road

Best Time to Visit: July to October

What to Know:

  • A small yet scenic waterfall surrounded by lush greenery.

Dao Kra Jai Waterfall

Khuan Kalong By road, ~1.5 hrs from Satun town

Best Time to Visit: Year-round (flows continuously)

What to Know:

  • A seven-tier waterfall in Khuan Kalong District, locally described as the tallest waterfall in Satun province.
  • About 1.5 hours' drive from Satun town; still a quiet, lesser-visited spot with a forested trail and natural swimming pools.

Lu Du Waterfall

Mueang Satun Hiking trail from Ao Pante park HQ, Ko Tarutao

Best Time to Visit: November to April (easier trail conditions)

What to Know:

  • Reached via a jungle hiking trail on Ko Tarutao island, starting near the park headquarters at Ao Pante.
  • Known more for its cool, deep natural swimming pool than a dramatic cascade—bring snorkel gear for the small freshwater fish.
  • Part of a national park day trip or overnight stay on Tarutao; check trail conditions with rangers before setting out.

Ya Roi Waterfall

Khuan Don By road, then a signed trail

Location: Q553+HR9, Wang Prachan, Khuan Don District, Satun 91160, Thailand

Details:

  • A secluded, multi-tiered waterfall in Thale Ban National Park with a natural pool, reached by a stepped trail.
  • Google rating: 4.5 stars.

Thara Sawan Waterfall Forest Park

Khuan Kalong By road, then a signed trail

Location: W3P2+GPX, Khuan Kalong, Khuan Kalong District, Satun 91130, Thailand

Details:

  • A small, quiet waterfall and stream with a camping area, shaded by forest.
  • Google rating: 4.5 stars.

Namtok Panan

Khuan Kalong By road, then a signed trail

Location: V556+64V, Thung Nui, Khuan Kalong District, Satun 91130, Thailand

Details:

  • A multi-level waterfall near the Thung Nui hot spring area, with several spots for swimming.
  • Google rating: 4.0 stars.

Thung Wa Waterfall

Thung Wa By road, then a signed trail

Location: 5W4V+63H, Thung Wa, Thung Wa District, Satun 92180, Thailand

Details:

  • A waterfall located in Thung Wa District, Satun.
  • Google rating: 4.5 stars.

Pirate Waterfall

Mueang Satun By road, then a signed trail

Location: G872+72C, Ko Tarutao, Mueang Satun District, Satun 91000, Thailand

Details:

  • A jungle waterfall on Ko Tarutao island reached via a roughly 35-45 minute hike, with several pools and levels.
  • Google rating: 4.4 stars.

Sai Chai Waterfall

Khuan Kalong By road, then a signed trail

Location: X27Q+5FQ, Khuan Kalong, Khuan Kalong District, Satun 91130, Thailand

Details:

  • A small natural waterfall about 1 km from the main road with a swimmable stream pool below the falls, near a local self-reliant community in Khuan Kalong district.
  • Google rating: 4.5 stars.

Rani Waterfall (Namtok Rani)

Khuan Don By road, then a signed trail

Location: P55C+XC8, Wang Prachan, Khuan Don District, Satun 91160, Thailand

Details:

  • A small waterfall inside Thale Ban National Park, an easy walk from the park headquarters, popular as a quiet spot to sit and cool off.
  • Google rating: 4.3 stars.
