Islands & beaches

Ko Lipe, Ko Adang, Ko Rawi and more—most reached by speedboat from Pak Bara pier.

Temples & mosque

Thai-Buddhist temples, a mosque, and Chinese shrines, mostly within Muang Satun district.

Caves & waterfalls

Phu Pha Phet's cave system and jungle waterfall trails make for a full inland day trip.

Planning tip: Pair one nature day (islands or waterfalls) with one culture day in town—most cultural sites sit close together, while island trips need a full day for the boat ride.
