Beaches

23 beaches across Satun's coastal districts, from the Dragon's Spine sandbar to sunrise and sunset viewpoints.

Beaches

Dragon Spine Beach (Hat San Lang Mangkon)

Mueang Satun Boat to the sandbar, tide-dependent

Best Time to Visit: During low tide (consult locals for timing)

What to Know:

  • A rare natural phenomenon where a sandbar emerges, allowing you to walk in the middle of the sea.

Pak Bara Beach

La-ngu By road, next to Pak Bara Pier

Best Time to Visit: Year-round

What to Know:

  • The main gateway to Satun’s islands.
  • Offers fresh seafood restaurants and peaceful seaside views.

Sunrise Beach

Mueang Satun Walk from Ko Lipe village

Best Time to Visit: November to April

What to Know:

  • Famous for spectacular sunrises and serenity.

Sunset Beach

Mueang Satun Walk from Ko Lipe village

Best Time to Visit: November to April

What to Know:

  • Offers stunning views of the sunset over the Andaman Sea.

North Point Beach (Bulow Beach), Koh Lipe

Mueang Satun By road, then a short walk to the shore

Location: Tambon Ko Tarutao, Amphoe Mueang Satun, Chang Wat Satun 91000, Thailand

Details:

  • Quiet beach at the northern tip of Koh Lipe's Sunrise Beach, popular for sunset views over the surrounding islands.
  • Google rating: 4.8 stars.

Pattaya Beach (Sunrise Beach), Koh Lipe

Mueang Satun By road, then a short walk to the shore

Location: Sunrise Beach, Ko Tarutao, Mueang Satun District, Satun 91000, Thailand

Details:

  • The main and most developed beach on Koh Lipe, lined with resorts, restaurants, and longtail boats, known for sunrise views.
  • Google rating: 4.5 stars.

Pantai Galah Beach, Koh Lipe

Mueang Satun By road, then a short walk to the shore

Location: Ko Lipe, Amphoe Mueang Satun, Chang Wat Satun, Thailand

Details:

  • A secluded, less-visited white-sand cove on Koh Lipe reached by a jungle trail or kayak, popular for snorkeling.
  • Google rating: 4.2 stars.

Maricilla Beach, Koh Lipe

Mueang Satun By road, then a short walk to the shore

Location: Ko Tarutao, Mueang Satun District, Satun 91000, Thailand

Details:

  • A shaded, less-crowded Koh Lipe beach good for snorkeling near coral walls just offshore.
  • Google rating: 4.8 stars.

Sai Khao Beach, Ko Rawi

Mueang Satun By road, then a short walk to the shore

Location: Ko Ra Wi, Amphoe Mueang Satun, Satun 91000, Thailand

Details:

  • A shaded rest-stop beach on Ko Rawi with turquoise water, used by inter-island snorkeling day tours.
  • Google rating: 4.8 stars.

Talo Sai Beach

La-ngu By road, then a short walk to the shore

Location: Tambon Pak Nam, Amphoe La-ngu, Chang Wat Satun 91110, Thailand

Details:

  • A quiet, partly eroded local beach near Pak Nam known for sunset viewing and its Malay-origin name meaning 'algae-covered bay'.
  • Google rating: 4.5 stars.

La-ngu Beach

La-ngu By road, then a short walk to the shore

Location: Tambon La-ngu, Amphoe La-ngu, Chang Wat Satun 91110, Thailand

Details:

  • A local coastal recreation spot in La-ngu town protected by a coastal sea wall, mostly used by nearby residents.
  • Google rating: 4.1 stars.

Rawai Beach

Thung Wa By road, then a short walk to the shore

Location: Ban Wang Tong area, Khon Khlan Subdistrict, Thung Wa District, Satun 91120, Thailand

Details:

  • A roughly 3.5 km pine-lined stretch of coastline within Mu Ko Phetra National Park, described locally as the longest beach in Satun Province.
  • Google rating: 4.1 stars.

Thung Saba Beach

Thung Wa By road, then a short walk to the shore

Location: Thung Bu Lang Subdistrict, Thung Wa District, Satun 91120, Thailand

Details:

  • A distinctive black-sand beach covered in shells, known for shellfish gathering at low tide and sunset views.
  • Google rating: 4.4 stars.

Ka Sing Beach (หาดกาสิงห์ / Kasing Beach)

La-ngu By road, then a short walk to the shore

Location: Ban Bo Chet Luk, La-ngu District, Satun, Thailand

Details:

  • A quiet, pine-tree-lined beach near Ban Bo Chet Luk in La-ngu district, popular for its sunset views.
  • Google rating: 4.2 stars.

Hua Hin Beach, Satun (ชายหาดหัวหิน)

La-ngu By road, then a short walk to the shore

Location: Tambon La-ngu, Amphoe La-ngu, Chang Wat Satun 91110, Thailand

Details:

  • A local beach near La-ngu town with a long fishing-village history, popular for camping and sunset views. Not to be confused with the famous Hua Hin resort town in Prachuap Khiri Khan province.
  • Google rating: 4.1 stars.

Toba Beach (หาดโต๊ะบะ)

La-ngu By road, then a short walk to the shore

Location: Pak Nam, La-ngu District, Satun 91110, Thailand

Details:

  • A national park beach within Mu Ko Phetra with soft white sand and fossil rock formations, accessible only during daytime hours.
  • Google rating: 4.7 stars.

Hin Lak Sri Beach (หาดหินหลากสี, multicolored stone beach)

La-ngu By road, then a short walk to the shore

Location: RQP2+GMJ, Pak Nam, La-ngu District, Satun 91110, Thailand

Details:

  • A beach within Mu Ko Phetra National Park known for its multicolored sandstone cliffs and view of Ko Tarutao in the distance.
  • Google rating: 4.4 stars.

Ban Bu Boi Pine Forest Beach (สวนสนทะเล บ้านบุโบย)

La-ngu By road, then a short walk to the shore

Location: Tambon Laem Son, Amphoe La-ngu, Chang Wat Satun 91110, Thailand

Details:

  • A grassy, pine-lined coastal picnic and camping spot in Laem Son subdistrict, La-ngu district.
  • Google rating: 4.5 stars.

Monkey Beach, Ko Tarutao

Mueang Satun By road, then a short walk to the shore

Location: Ko Tarutao, Mueang Satun District, Satun 91000, Thailand

Details:

  • A white-sand beach on Ko Tarutao island with swings over the water, a common lunch stop on island-hopping tours; home to a resident troop of monkeys.
  • Google rating: 4.5 stars.

Ao Pante Malacca Beach (Punte Malaka)

Mueang Satun By road, then a short walk to the shore

Location: Ao Pante Malacca, Ko Tarutao, Tarutao National Park, Mueang Satun District, Satun Province

Details:

  • The long sandy beach fronting the Tarutao National Park headquarters at Ao Pante Malacca on Ko Tarutao, roughly 3.1 km long, with the park's visitor center, restaurant, campground and pier. It is the main arrival point for boats from Pak Bara and the base for exploring Tarutao's trails, caves and Toe Boo Cliff viewpoint.

Mo Lae Bay Beach

Mueang Satun By road, then a short walk to the shore

Location: Ao Molae, Ko Tarutao, Tarutao National Park, Mueang Satun District, Satun Province

Details:

  • A quiet, largely undeveloped beach in Ao Molae bay on the east side of Ko Tarutao, about 590 m of pale sand backed by forested hills, reachable only by boat. Ranked among the top beaches on the island for its clear water and minimal development.

Ko Tarutao East Beach (Ao Molae)

Mueang Satun By road, then a short walk to the shore

Location: Ao Molae area, Ko Tarutao, Mueang Satun District, Satun Province (approx. 6°39'47"N 99°37'42"E)

Details:

  • A roughly 500 m stretch of pale sandy shore near Ao Molae on Ko Tarutao, backed by forested hills, with a gradual sandy entry into clear water. Reached only by boat; minimal facilities.

Desperate Beach (Koh Pak Nam)

La-ngu By road, then a short walk to the shore

Location: Koh Pak Nam Island, near Pak Bara, La-ngu District, Satun Province

Details:

  • A small, secluded beach on Koh Pak Nam island near Pak Bara, about 130 m long with turquoise water and cliff-and-forest surroundings. Reached only by boat; a quiet spot for kayaking, on the same small island as Toba Beach.
