8 shrines around Satun province, local places of worship for the Chinese community.

8 spots listed

Shrine

Po Che Keng Shrine

Mueang Satun By road, in Satun town

Location:Phiman Subdistrict, Mueang Satun District, Satun Province

Details:

  • Po Che Keng Shrine is a local shrine dedicated to traditional deities and spirits. It is an important spiritual site for the local community, where people gather to pray for blessings, protection, and good fortune. The shrine is a fascinating spot for visitors interested in Thai folk religion and the cultural practices of the region.

San Chao Pho Che Keng (Po-Jeh-Geng Shrine)

Mueang Satun By road

Location: 51 Samanthapradit, Tambon Phiman, Amphoe Mueang Satun, Chang Wat Satun 91000

Details:

  • A Chinese shrine (Ah-am) in Satun town, popular for Chinese New Year and local festivals.
  • Google rating: 4.8 stars.

San Chao Pho Lak Mueang Satun (City Pillar Shrine)

Mueang Satun By road

Location: M38F+WR5, 406, Tambon Khlong Khut, Amphoe Mueang Satun, Chang Wat Satun 91000

Details:

  • The city pillar shrine of Satun province.
  • Google rating: 4.6 stars.

San Chao Mae Thapthim

Mueang Satun By road

Location: 529 Moo 3, Tambon Khlong Khut, Amphoe Mueang Satun, Chang Wat Satun 91000

Details:

  • A Chinese shrine dedicated to Chao Mae Thapthim in Khlong Khut, Mueang Satun.
  • Google rating: 4.5 stars.

San Chao Kuang Hok Keng

La-ngu By road

Location: VQMP+JC8, Kamphaeng, La-ngu District, Satun 91110, Thailand

Details:

  • An old Chinese (Taoist) shrine in Kamphaeng subdistrict of La-ngu district, valued locally as a site of Chinese art and culture.
