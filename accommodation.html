Find your stay

Most stays cluster around Muang Satun town and the Pak Bara pier area in La-ngu — the gateway for ferries to Ko Lipe, Ko Tarutao, and Ko Bulon Lae — but we've mapped real, currently-operating places across 6 of Satun's 7 districts, including small rafting resorts near Wang Sai Thong Waterfall and bungalows out on Ko Bulon island. Use the district filter below to browse by area. Manang district (home to the Emerald Tunnel cave) has no hotels of its own; the closest options are in neighbouring Khuan Kalong, about 20–30 minutes away. Ratings shown are a live snapshot from Google — a few newer or smaller places have very few reviews yet, so check current feedback before booking.
