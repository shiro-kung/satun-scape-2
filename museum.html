Satun's provincial museum plus other museums and landmarks worth a stop.

Museum & landmarks

Satun National Museum – Kuden Mansion

Mueang Satun By road, walkable in Satun town centre

Location:Satun Thani Road, Mueang District, Satun Province

Details:

  • Kuden Mansion, located in Satun, is a historic building that offers a glimpse into the region's colonial past. The mansion was once home to a prominent local family or official and now serves as a museum or cultural site. It is a must-visit for history enthusiasts, offering insights into the heritage of Satun through its preserved architecture and exhibits.

Phu Pha Pet Cave Ray of Emerald Tunnel

Manang By road

Location:Moo 9, Pa Pon Village, Palm Pattana Subdistrict, Manang District, Satun Province

Details:

  • The Ray of Emerald Tunnel is a natural formation where sunlight filters through the forest or rock, creating an ethereal emerald glow. It is a picturesque and tranquil location, perfect for nature lovers and photographers. The tunnel is part of Satun's eco-tourism offerings and provides visitors with a unique experience of Thailand's natural beauty.

Thung Wa Ancient Elephant Museum

Thung Wa By road

Location: 32/5 Highway 416, Tambon Thung Wa, Amphoe Thung Wa, Chang Wat Satun 91120

Details:

  • A museum displaying fossils of ancient elephants (Stegodon) discovered in the area, part of the Satun UNESCO Global Geopark.
  • Google rating: 4.1 stars.

Satun Clock Tower

Mueang Satun By road

Location: J388+376, Thanon Ruangritjaroon, Tambon Phiman, Amphoe Mueang Satun, Chang Wat Satun 91000, Thailand

Details:

  • A landmark clock tower at a roundabout in central Satun town, opposite the Central Mosque, widely cited as a reference point/symbol of the city.
  • Google rating: 4.8 stars.

Satun Street Art

Mueang Satun By road

Location: 168 Thanon Satun Thani, Tambon Phiman, Amphoe Mueang Satun, Chang Wat Satun 91000, Thailand

Details:

  • An open-air collection of murals along Satun Thani Road in Satun town depicting local culture and identity.
  • Google rating: 4.5 stars.

Satun Geopark Gateway

Khuan Don By road

Location: R36V+GP3, Route 406, Tambon Khuan Don, Amphoe Khuan Don, Chang Wat Satun 91160, Thailand

Details:

  • A gateway landmark and photo stop with statues marking entry to the Satun UNESCO Global Geopark area, on the road between Satun and Hat Yai.
  • Google rating: 4.2 stars.

Bokha Hill Viewpoint

Khuan Don By road

Location: Ban Thang Ngo, Tambon Khuan Sato, Amphoe Khuan Don, Chang Wat Satun 91160, Thailand

Details:

  • A rocky hill in Khuan Don district with a short climb leading to a 360-degree viewpoint over the surrounding mountains and town.
  • Google rating: 4.4 stars.

Satun Provincial Cultural Office

Mueang Satun By road

Location: 350 Thanon Khuha Prawet, Tambon Phiman, Amphoe Mueang Satun, Chang Wat Satun 91000, Thailand

Details:

  • The provincial cultural affairs office in Satun town, which hosts cultural exhibits and events on local heritage.
  • Google rating: 5.0 stars.
